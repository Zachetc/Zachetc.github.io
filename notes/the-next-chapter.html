The next chapter is a contribution.

The capabilities, responsibilities, and impact I want to grow into.

By Zachary Amachee · October 2026

I’m building toward a career where careful analysis and practical technology help people make better decisions. My next step is an opportunity to contribute to that work as part of a team.

The contribution I want to make now.

I’m interested in early-career roles in data analytics, business intelligence, and business systems. Financial services and business operations particularly interest me because they bring together information, processes, and decisions with concrete consequences.

I want to help prepare reliable data, investigate useful questions, improve reporting, and communicate findings clearly. My portfolio is a way to demonstrate those interests through work a reviewer can explore.

The capabilities I want to build.

My goals include deepening my SQL and Python skills, learning more about data engineering and cloud systems, and developing stronger judgment about how technical choices serve a business need. Working with experienced teammates would also help me understand the standards and tradeoffs of a production environment.

What future success would mean.

Over time, I want to earn responsibility for larger projects, become someone colleagues trust to explain a difficult problem, and help other people grow. I would measure that progress through useful work, reliable delivery, and the confidence of the people who depend on me.

Those are goals I’m working toward. The next chapter begins with the opportunity to contribute, keep learning, and follow through.
