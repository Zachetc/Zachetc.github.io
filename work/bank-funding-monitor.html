Bank Funding Monitor

Connecting public financial data, a relational database, and an interpretable dashboard to make a large banking dataset easier to investigate.

The business question

Which banks in a comparable asset range warrant further funding analysis? The project focuses on banks with $1 billion to under $10 billion in assets and looks for a combination of declining deposits and relatively high net lending ratios.

The aim is to create a transparent starting point for review, with the underlying records and calculations available for inspection.

From source to analysis

The Python pipeline extracts quarterly FDIC BankFind records, validates identifiers and financial fields, and loads bank and period dimensions into SQLite. SQL joins match each bank to its exact prior-year quarter, while pandas supports peer comparisons and analysis.

  • Preserved raw responses, request URLs, retrieval dates, and checksums for traceability.
  • Separated missing values from zero and protected ratio calculations against invalid denominators.
  • Combined negative deposit growth with a same-quarter peer threshold for net loans relative to deposits.
  • Created CSV exports, a packaged Tableau workbook, and a companion dashboard image.

What the project produced

67,451bank-quarter records
4,729banking entities
40banks in the June 2026 review queue

For June 2026, 889 peer banks had a valid prior-year deposit comparison. Of those, 159 reported lower deposits, and 40 also met the lending-ratio threshold. The repository includes the review queue, business memo, sensitivity analysis, and 14 automated tests.

How to interpret it

The review queue is an analytical screen. It does not establish financial distress, predict failures, or measure realized business impact.

Deposit balances can change because of mergers, transfers, business models, and other funding sources. Asset size alone does not make banks identical. The repository also notes that native Tableau Desktop/Public opening and rendering have not been verified.
